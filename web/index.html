SafeStop Relay

Alexa+ MCP server

A quiet roadside SOS for travellers in India. Say a harmless safe word to Alexa (or hold one button) and your trusted contacts are alerted at once, with honest delivery states, safe steps for five roadside situations, unverified safe places from OpenStreetMap, and an opt-in relay to SafeStop users within 1 km.

Alexa+ / MCP clients: POST /mcp (Streamable HTTP, protocol 2025-11-25).

Traveller, trusted contact: use the private links printed by the server.

Nearby traveller: /nearby
